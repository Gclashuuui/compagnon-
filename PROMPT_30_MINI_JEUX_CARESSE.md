# Prompt — 30 icônes pour les mini-jeux de caresse

Copie le bloc ci-dessous dans Astra, ChatGPT Images ou Midjourney, avec une
capture du nouveau livre comme référence de style. Les icônes serviront au HUD
Minecraft : elles doivent donc rester très lisibles en tout petit.

```text
Create a production-ready pixel-art icon collection for a Minecraft 1.21.1
fantasy companion mod called “Compagnon”. The icons represent 30 gentle petting
mini-games inside an elegant magical relationship journal.

ART DIRECTION
- authentic hand-pixelled fantasy UI, not vector art and not smooth digital art;
- designed on a strict 16x16 pixel grid, presented enlarged with nearest-neighbor
  pixels only;
- strong, instantly recognizable silhouettes, 1-pixel dark outline, restrained
  interior detail, no anti-aliasing, no gradients, no blur;
- warm parchment, muted rose, soft gold, leather brown and a few sage-green
  success accents;
- tender and magical, never childish, never modern-mobile-game style;
- all icons share the same visual weight, outline thickness and centered scale;
- genuinely transparent background, no square tile behind the icon;
- no text, letters, numbers, labels, checkmarks, frames, watermark or shadow
  outside the silhouette;
- one main symbol per icon, with at most one tiny magical accent.

DELIVERABLES
1. A clean 6-column by 5-row contact sheet, in the exact order below, with large
   transparent gaps between icons.
2. The same 30 icons as separate transparent PNG files, named
   caresse_01.png through caresse_30.png.
3. Keep every final sprite visually valid when reduced to exactly 16x16 pixels.
   Do not place any important detail thinner than one final pixel.

THE 30 ICONS — EXACT ORDER
01 Trois battements — three small heart pulses, rising gently.
02 Cœur régulier — one heart surrounded by two balanced rhythm marks.
03 Double douceur — two overlapping soft hearts.
04 Petit tempo — three light golden beats, abstract and delicate.
05 Souffle tranquille — one calm curling breath embracing a tiny heart.
06 Ronde tendre — two curved strokes circling a heart.
07 Éclat complice — a heart and one four-point magical sparkle.
08 Geste de velours — one silky curved stroke with a tiny heart-shaped end.
09 Souffle doré — a warm curling breath carrying one gold mote.
10 Pulsation magique — a glowing heart with a single pulse line.

11 Ruban soyeux — a flowing ribbon making one calm S curve.
12 Longue caresse — a gentle hand stroke represented by one long curved trail.
13 Toucher léger — one delicate fingertip-like curve with a tiny gold glint.
14 Chaleur partagée — two small warm lights touching in the center.
15 Geste paisible — an open relaxed hand with one soft motion line.
16 Frisson heureux — three short rising shimmer marks beside a heart.
17 Nuage calme — one small soft cloud cradling a heart.
18 Main rassurante — a simple hand resting above a small heart.
19 Douce habitude — two parallel curved strokes, calm and regular.
20 Lien apaisé — two ribbon ends joined by a small luminous knot.

21 Écho du cœur — one heart followed by two smaller echo silhouettes.
22 Petit rituel — three tiny golden motes arranged in a gentle sequence.
23 Geste secret — a closed scroll curl with one discreet sparkle.
24 Signe complice — two mirrored hand-like curves meeting in the center.
25 Danse des mains — two elegant opposing motion arcs.
26 Code affectueux — three simple rune-like dots joined by a heart line.
27 Mémoire tendre — a small memory ribbon wrapped around a heart.
28 Suite enchantée — three different magical symbols connected in sequence.
29 Clin d’œil magique — one friendly stylized eye and a tiny sparkle.
30 Promesse silencieuse — two small linked rings beneath a heart.

CONSISTENCY CHECK
Every icon must read clearly at thumbnail size. Avoid realistic hands, detailed
animals, human faces, UI cards, busy backgrounds and duplicated symbols. Output
only the icon collection and the separate transparent sprites.
```

Les états **survolé**, **réussi** et **raté** seront colorés directement par le
mod. Il ne faut donc pas demander 90 images différentes : les 30 icônes de base
suffisent.
